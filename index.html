About Me

Learning QA through practice, not theory alone.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering student with a strong interest in Software Quality Assurance. I am building my QA foundation through manual testing, SDLC, STLC, test scenario and test case design, bug reporting, test execution, retesting, and regression testing.

Alongside structured practice projects, I am also gaining exposure to a live web application where testing happens in a real development environment. My programming background includes C, C++, Java, and Python, with MySQL for database work.
